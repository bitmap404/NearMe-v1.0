Your privacy, clearly explained

NearMe uses Telegram login data to recognize visitors and optional GPS to help find nearby shops. This is a starter privacy notice. The app owner must add their contact details, jurisdiction-specific disclosures, and retention rules before a public launch.

Telegram account information

When you open the Mini App inside Telegram, the server verifies Telegram-signed login data. It stores your Telegram numeric ID, display name, username if available, first and most recent visit times, and total visit count. The app owner can view recent records using the private bot /visitors command.

Website login and sessions

When you open the NearMe website directly, selecting Continue with Telegram redirects you to Telegram authorization through OpenID Connect. The backend validates the signed Telegram ID token and creates an access session. The website stores a random access token in your browser storage for up to 7 days, unless you log out earlier. The backend stores only a hash of that session token along with account identifiers and an expiration time. Telegram authentication is provided using an associated bot identity, but you do not need to send messages to the bot.

Visitor dashboards and activity tracking

While NearMe is open, it sends an authenticated activity signal approximately every 45 seconds to the server. The server stores the Telegram ID, name (when available), and last NearMe activity time. Owner and specifically authorized staff can view an estimated active-member list (recent activity within 2 minutes), recent login/logout events, and visitor counts. This does not reveal or track a person's Telegram-wide online status. Ordinary visitors see only historical aggregate counts delayed by at least 30 minutes, never identities or the active-user list. These signals do not include GPS or IP addresses as part of the dashboard database. The owner should define a retention schedule before launch.

Staff accounts and audit records

Approved NearMe administrators and content moderators are granted individual roles using their numeric Telegram IDs. The Primary Owner can activate, suspend, or remove staff access. NearMe records staff IDs, roles, administrative changes, shop edits, approval decisions, and timestamps in an internal activity log. These logs are visible to the Primary Owner, not ordinary visitors.

Location

Location is requested only if you tap Use my location and grant permission. For a search, your device sends GPS coordinates to the NearMe server, which calculates distances to advertisements entered by authorized NearMe staff. GPS coordinates are not sent to a place-search service and are not stored in the visitor database. Normal network infrastructure may still process IP addresses and connection metadata.

Shop advertisements

The shop names, addresses, photos, prices, WhatsApp contacts and Telegram links displayed in the app are managed by approved NearMe administrators and content moderators. Selecting an external contact button sends you to WhatsApp or Telegram, where their own policies apply. The shop pin may represent an administrator-verified approximate location rather than a registered Google Maps business.

Maps and saved places

Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap when you open the map. Walking directions open Google Maps. Saved places remain in your device's local browser storage; they are not sent to the visitor database.

Questions and deletion

Before public launch, the owner should provide a contact email or Telegram handle and a process for visitors to request removal of their recorded account information. Do not publish this starter notice unchanged for real customers.
